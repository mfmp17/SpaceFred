date: 2026-11-25
summary: Tighter Dwindle and resize behavior, cleaner native-tab and window-memory handling, more predictable cross-display focus, and a clearer Settings shortcut recorder.
---
## Improvements
- Fullscreen Layout survives hiding (`⌘H`) or minimizing (`⌘M`) every tile on a workspace, together or one at a time, and resumes when any of those windows returns.
- `Option+Tab` remembers the floating or pinned window left behind on another display by directional focus or a workspace switch, and brings focus back from a pinned sibling on another display.
- A live `pin` rule floats and shows a matching parked tile straight away without requiring a visit to its workspace, and matches parked floats against their full title rather than the 512-character memory copy.

## Fixes

### Tiling & Dwindle
- A routed window in Dwindle splits the workspace's focused tile, a close and open arriving together split the survivor by its post-close space, and a nested manual split keeps its direction when one tile remains and another joins.
- Growing or shrinking a tile keeps the untouched seam when a sibling first reveals its minimum size, and minimum-size refits write their seams exactly so moved tiles no longer overlap.
- Switching native tabs right after resizing a split keeps the resized tile width.
- Held drags keep their target across edge cases: a cross-display drop stays under the pointer when its workspace moves, a gap change mid-drag still reaches the seam after layout toggles, and portrait displays' side-band menu bar and Dock strip ends no longer arm the dock.
- Fullscreen Layout drags released a point or two off restore the exact single-window cap, and leaving Fullscreen Layout right after a drag starts keeps the drag ignored.
- Changing finger count mid-swipe no longer drops the trackpad Space-switch hold.

### Native Tabs & Window Memory
- An ignored same-app window no longer sends a native tab to its route workspace, and `move-tab` detaches an AppKit tab even when an ignore rule matches the tab left behind.
- Queued `move-tab --follow` commands detach the next tab from the source group in order.
- Switching a native tab no longer delays saving an unchanged window arrangement, and a one-part utility title no longer keeps a shared browser suffix as window identity.
- A first-window route stays consumed after a live `float` rule floats its window, and resuming from pause releases a restored window that an ignore rule now matches.

### Focus & Workspaces
- Reselecting a visible workspace no longer restores a minimized or hidden float, and moving a workspace leaves minimized or hidden windows on the revealed workspace alone.
- Recent Focus judges tile borders by where capped windows actually sit on screen.
- Scratchpad and workspace steps triggered from a dragged float or a pin centered in a menu bar strip target the display that window is on, and `scratchpad mark` hides a focused pinned float.
- Reassigning a visible workspace to another display brings its floating windows with it.

### Settings & Shortcuts
- The shortcut recorder records modified `Escape` chords instead of cancelling, refuses keypad keys instead of binding their main-row twin, refuses a `Shift` chord already bound under its typed character, and keeps a refusal reason visible after the keys come up.
- Settings › Shortcuts lists the workspace keys that still run when a `defaults = false` table is rejected, and names the built-in shortcut still running on a workspace letter enabled after startup.
- Saving a Workspace HUD or built-in-display setting no longer hides a config edit made just before it.
- CLI rule and config removals drop only the targeted duplicate's comments and preserve comments separated by a blank line.
